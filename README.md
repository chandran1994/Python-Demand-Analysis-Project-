# Demand Management & Sales Forecasting Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on **Demand Management Analytics** using customer sales data to uncover purchasing patterns, demand trends, and product movement behaviour across regions and customer segments.


The project demonstrates how data analytics can support:
- Demand forecasting
- Inventory planning
- Regional demand balancing
- Product prioritization
- Customer-driven supply chain strategies

---

# Business Problem

Retail and supply chain companies frequently struggle with:
- Demand uncertainty
- Overstocking
- Stockouts
- Poor inventory allocation
- Inaccurate forecasting
- Regional imbalance in product demand

Without proper demand analysis:
- Warehouses become inefficient
- Inventory carrying costs rise
- Customer service levels decline
- Revenue opportunities are lost

This project focuses on understanding:
- Which customer segments drive demand
- Which products move fastest
- Which regions generate the highest demand
- How demographic behavior impacts demand patterns

---

- Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on sales data

# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 1. Customer Demand by Gender

### Findings
- Female customers generated the majority of total sales demand.
- Women represented the highest purchasing frequency and spending behavior.

### Demand Management Insight
Understanding dominant customer demand segments improves:
- Demand forecasting accuracy
- Product allocation planning
- Inventory prioritization

---

## 2. Age-Based Demand Analysis

### Findings
Highest demand originated from:
- Age group 26–35
- Followed by:
  - 36–45
  - 18–25

### Demand Management Insight
Age-based demand segmentation supports:
- Seasonal demand planning
- Product mix optimization
- Targeted inventory deployment

---

## 3. Regional Demand Analysis

### Findings
Top demand-generating states:
- Uttar Pradesh
- Maharashtra
- Karnataka

### Demand Management Insight
Regional demand analysis improves:
- Distribution planning
- Warehouse allocation
- Transportation efficiency
- Regional inventory balancing

---

## 4. Marital Status Demand Behavior

### Findings
- Married women contributed the highest overall purchasing demand.

### Demand Management Insight
Behavioral segmentation improves:
- Forecasting consistency
- Product demand prediction
- Inventory positioning strategies

---

## 5. Occupation-Based Demand Analysis

### Findings
Highest spending professions:
- IT
- Healthcare
- Aviation

### Demand Management Insight
Occupation-based purchasing behavior helps identify:
- Stable demand clusters
- High-value customer groups
- Premium product demand

---

## 6. Product Category Demand Analysis

### Findings
Top-selling categories:
- Food
- Clothing
- Electronics

### Demand Management Insight
Product-level demand analysis supports:
- SKU prioritization
- Replenishment optimization
- Safety stock planning
- Demand forecasting

The analysis identified:

# Business Recommendations

## 1. Demand-Driven Inventory Planning
Increase stock levels for:
- High-demand product categories
- High-performing regions
- Fast-moving SKUs

---

## 2. Improve Forecasting Accuracy
Use customer demographic patterns to improve:
- Demand prediction
- Seasonal forecasting
- Replenishment planning

---

## 3. Regional Distribution Optimization
Deploy inventory closer to:
- Uttar Pradesh
- Maharashtra
- Karnataka

This reduces:
- Delivery lead times
- Logistics costs
- Stockout risks

---

## 4. Product Prioritization Strategy
Focus supply chain resources on:
- Food
- Clothing
- Electronics

These categories drive the majority of demand.

---

## 5. Customer-Centric Demand Planning
Align forecasting and inventory planning with:
- High-value customer segments
- Purchasing trends
- Demand seasonality
